About Us

Shiv Saree Emporium (Frontier Wale) is a wholesale ladies suit business in Katra Neel, Chandni Chowk, Delhi. We serve retailers, boutiques and buyers looking for occasion wear and ready-made suit collections.

Our range includes handwork, party wear, bridal and ready-made styles. We focus on fabric selection, embroidery, finishing and practical variety for wholesale buyers.
